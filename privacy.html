Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

About the application

Fortnite Shorts Auto is a personal desktop application used exclusively by its developer to prepare and upload original gameplay videos to their own YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube data

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 and the YouTube Data API v3 to upload videos to the authorized YouTube account.

Authentication credentials are stored locally on the developer's computer and are not shared with third parties.

Data storage

Video files, authentication tokens and upload history are stored locally. The application does not operate a remote database or collect data from other users.

Data sharing

The application does not sell personal data or share it with advertisers. Videos and necessary authentication information are transmitted to Google services as required for authorized YouTube uploads.

Access and revocation

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Contact

For questions about this application, visit the creator's YouTube channel.
