Privacy Policy - Fortnite Shorts Auto

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Fortnite Shorts Auto is a personal desktop application used only by its developer to upload their own gameplay recordings to their own YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

This application uses YouTube API Services.

By using this application, the developer agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service and acknowledges the Google Privacy Policy.

Data accessed

The application requests permission to upload videos to the authorized YouTube account using Google OAuth 2.0. It does not request access to unrelated Google services.

Data storage and sharing

Video files, OAuth authentication tokens and upload history are stored locally on the developer's computer. Authentication data is not sold or shared with advertisers. Information required for authorized uploads is transmitted to Google and YouTube.

Data retention and deletion

Local authentication tokens and upload history are retained until the developer deletes them. The developer can delete these local files at any time. Uploaded videos can be managed or deleted through YouTube Studio.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked through Google Account settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized uploads until authorization is granted again.

Contact

This is a private, non-commercial application. For inquiries, visit the developer's YouTube channel.
